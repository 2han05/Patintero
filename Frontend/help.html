How to Play

Patintero is a traditional Filipino street game. Players try to cross the lines without being tagged by the defenders. Use PowerUp to become invincible.

Player 1: W A S D, PowerUp: Q

Player 2: I J K L, PowerUp: U

Player 3: Arrow Keys, PowerUp: 1

Easy Mode: Defenders move slower.

Hard Mode: Defenders move faster.

Note

There is no easy mode in 3 players because it's too easy.

All modes are beatable there is just a hidden technique on how to pass every mode goodluck!.
